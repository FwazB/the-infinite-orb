# Visual reference brief

## Source and scope

Private, nonshipping research informed the vehicle brief. The public documentation deliberately omits account associations, conversation provenance, attachment names and local machine paths. Reference images are not included in the public source or runtime bundle.

## Observed vehicle forms

- Early car references: short rounded bodies, substantial tires and unusually tall upright glass cabins. These are secondary references, not the selected vehicle.
- Selected enclosed motorcycle: broad rear tire, tall transparent bubble/fairing, rounded white bodywork, red rear lights and visible mechanical/exhaust detail.

The references do not establish exact canonical names, dimensions, unseen geometry or a flight mechanism. Unseen details require design or further research rather than being presented as observed.

## Approved direction

Fwaz chose the enclosed motorcycle without a character and authorized the local prototype. The current procedural blockout is provisional, not a finished Blender recreation. Final proportions, palette and styling remain subject to design approval.

## Proposed mood

Preserve playful, rounded mechanical forms and a calm adventurous mood. Reference scenery supplies mood cues, not permission to replace the Supreme Kai setting. Final scenery and palette remain design decisions.

## Storage and publication

Private references remain in an ignored directory, separate from runtime assets. The asset register covers the procedural motorcycle and scene generators. Neither viewing reference art nor independently modeling geometry establishes rights in a recognizable third-party design. No reference image is a shipping asset.
